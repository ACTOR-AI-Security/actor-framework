# Roadmap

## v0.2

- Control-level mappings: NIST Cyber AI Profile (IR 8596), NIST COSAiS overlays, CSA AI Controls Matrix, ISO/IEC 42001, MITRE ATLAS, ISACA AAISM job practice, the EU AI Act, the OWASP Agent Control Standard, the SANS Critical AI Security Guidelines, the SANS AI Security Maturity Model, and the AI Defense Matrix.
- Evidence-based assessment method: a rating for each control based on documented evidence of implementation, not self-report.
- Assessment columns in the control data: evidence examples and test guidance, typical implementation mode for SaaS, configured, and built deployments, sub-requirement IDs for lettered parts, and organization-defined parameters.
- Public release under CC BY 4.0, with license and contribution terms reviewed by counsel.
- Update OWASP references to the LLM Top 10 2026 edition.
- Implementation guidance and evidence examples for every Foundational control.
- Named peer reviewers and contributors.
- Results from two or three pilot organizations.
- Decide whether to keep Harden and Defend as separate functions.

## Later

- Assessment questionnaire built on the v0.2 assessment method.
- Implementation notes for common AI platforms, kept separate from the tool-neutral controls.
- Evaluate hosting with an established community, such as an OWASP project.
