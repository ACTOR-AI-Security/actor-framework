# Use of the ACTOR name

The framework content is licensed under CC BY 4.0. You can use and adapt it freely with attribution.

The name "ACTOR" as applied to this AI security framework identifies the official version maintained in this repository. To avoid confusion:

- You may say your work is "based on" or "adapted from" ACTOR, with attribution.
- You may cite ACTOR control IDs, such as ACTOR DEL-ID-01, when referring to the official controls.
- Please do not publish a modified version under the ACTOR name, or imply that a modified version is official or endorsed, without permission.

Questions about use of the name can be raised through an issue in this repository.
