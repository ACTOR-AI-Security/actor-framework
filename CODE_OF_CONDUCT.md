# Code of conduct

ACTOR is a professional community project. Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas and wording, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Keep discussion on topic and useful to others reading later.
- Respect confidentiality. Do not share anyone's confidential information, including your employer's or clients'.
- Disclose relevant affiliations when recommending a product, vendor, or service.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Spam, advertising, or self-promotion unrelated to the discussion.
- Publishing others' private information.

The maintainer may edit, hide, or remove comments that do not follow this code, and may block participants who repeatedly ignore it. Report concerns by contacting the maintainer.
